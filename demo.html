How it works

From word list to bingo in 30 seconds

No accounts. No setup. The card is the link.

1

2

Configure

Choose 3×3, 4×4, or 5×5. Toggle a free space. Set win condition from first line to full blackout.

3

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built for sharing. Runs entirely in the browser.

🔗

The card is the link

Entire card state encoded in the URL hash. No database, no expiry — works forever.

🎲

Unique per player

Each link is a different subset and shuffle of your items. No two cards are the same.

💾

State survives refresh

Click state saved to localStorage keyed by card seed. Reload and your marks are still there.

🏆

Win image export

Canvas-rendered PNG with player name, card seed, and UTC timestamp. Provably yours.

📋

Host view

One URL to preview and distribute all cards at a glance. Copy or open any card in one click.

⚙️

Configurable wins

Set win condition from first line to full blackout. Works with 3×3, 4×4, and 5×5 grids.
